MoneyBoss Drive is a Private, Secure and Offline Vehicle Cost Tracker
Take control of what your vehicle really costs you with MoneyBoss Drive – a fast, private and easy-to-use Vehicle Cost Tracker built for everyday drivers.

MoneyBoss Drive works completely offline. No account to create, no cloud storage required for everyday use. Your vehicle data stays on your device, giving you complete control over your privacy.

Whether you're tracking fuel, maintenance, insurance or daily trips, MoneyBoss Drive helps you understand exactly what your vehicle costs with a clear dashboard and real Cost/km figures.

PRIVATE & SECURE BY DESIGN
Your vehicle data belongs only to you:
• MoneyBoss Drive never requires an account to use the app, except optional Google Backup and Compare
• There are no unnecessary permissions and no cloud dependency for everyday use
• Compare's Refresh and Reports are free to use by watching a short non-personalized Ad. Pro removes the ads entirely.
Secure your data with:
• PIN protection
• Fingerprint or Face Unlock
• Automatic app lock after repeated incorrect PIN attempts
• Your vehicle information stays on your device unless you choose to back up your data or opt into Compare

MULTIPLE LANGUAGES & CURRENCIES
• Supports English, Español, Português, Français and हिन्दी.
• Over 150 currencies supported.

TRACK EVERY VEHICLE COST
• Log Fuel, Maintenance, Accessories and Income in seconds
• Add any number of vehicles and swipe the vehicle images to switch between vehicles
• Track Service, Papers (insurance, tax, permit) and Charges (tolls, parking, fines) separately
• Record trips with true brim-to-brim mileage
• Search and filter your entire history instantly
• Edit any entry anytime from the recent list

UNDERSTAND YOUR VEHICLE'S REAL COST
• Clear dashboard with Total Cost/km and Running Cost/km
• See Net Profit/km once you log income — know if you're really making money
• Automatic resale valuation from depreciation or your own estimate
• Upcoming maintenance reminders by date or odometer, right on the dashboard
• Every figure recalculates fresh from your logs — no stale totals

BACKUP & RESTORE
• Never worry about losing your data
• Manual encrypted local backup & restore
• Automatic Google backup & restore protected by your Google account, 4 weekly snapshots kept

UNLOCK MORE
Watch a quick rewarded Ad to refresh your Compare rank or unlock Reports & PDF export.
Or upgrade to Pro to skip the ads entirely — every other feature stays free either way.

COMPARE WITH FRIENDS
See how your vehicle really stacks up.
• Compare your cost, mileage and profit against other drivers in your country
• Invite friends directly, or get matched with someone at random
• Rank by Total Cost/km, Running Cost/km, Mileage or Net Profit
• Only your comparison figures are ever shared — never your raw records

REPORTS & PDF EXPORT
Analyse your costs with powerful filters.
• Filter by date range, or compare two vehicles side by side
• Switch freely between both views once unlocked
• Export detailed PDF reports
