Getting Started
How do I set up the app?

On first launch, enter your name and set a 4-digit PIN. That's it — no sign-up or account is required to use the app.

How do I add my first vehicle?

Go to the Garage tab and tap Add Vehicle. Fill in its specifications, tap Save, then fill in Purchase Details and tap Save.

How do I change my name?

Go to Profile → Name and edit it there.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Profile → Language and pick from the list.

How do I enable biometrics?

Go to Profile and turn on Biometrics. You'll still be asked for your PIN occasionally — biometrics is a shortcut, not a replacement for it.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Go to Profile → Reminder and pick a time. You'll get a daily nudge to log today's expenses and refresh your Compare rank.

How do I switch between light and dark theme?

Go to Profile → Theme and choose System, Light, or Dark.

Garage & Vehicles
How many vehicles can I add?

As many as you like — adding vehicles is free, with no limit.

How do I switch between vehicles?

Swipe the vehicle picker at the top of Home, Add, Garage, or Compare. Switching there updates the active vehicle everywhere else in the app too.

What's the difference between archiving and deleting a vehicle?

Archiving (open the vehicle in Garage → Archive) hides it from your active list but keeps every record — restore it anytime from Profile → Data → Archived Vehicles. Deleting (Profile → Data → Delete Vehicles, PIN-protected) permanently removes the vehicle and everything logged against it, and can't be undone.

What is Purpose (Personal/Commercial)?

A label for how you use the vehicle. It only affects Compare — a Commercial vehicle with real income logged gets a small 🚕 marker next to its name there.

Can I change a vehicle's Country, Currency, Odometer Unit, or Fuel Volume Unit later?

Yes, from Garage → Edit Vehicle. These are independent settings — changing one doesn't change the others — so set each to match how you actually use the vehicle.

Does the app support electric vehicles?

Yes. Choose Electric as the fuel type and fuel is tracked in kWh instead of litres — Mileage and cost figures adjust automatically.

Logging
What can I log for a vehicle?

Fuel, Maintenance, Accessories, and Income — each has its own tab under Add. Trips get their own tab too (see the next section).

What counts as Maintenance?

Three groups: Service (periodic service, parts replacement, repairs, wash, tow), Papers (insurance, emission, fitness, permit, tax), and Charges (toll, pass, parking, fines, driver wage). Each group has its own "Other" category for anything that doesn't fit.

Can I log income for a vehicle?

Yes, under Add → Income — useful if you drive for a living. Once you've logged any income, Net Profit appears on Home and Compare.

How do I edit or delete a log entry?

Tap it in that tab's Recent list — it loads back into the form, with a delete option there too.

Does logging require an internet connection?

No. Every log — Fuel, Maintenance, Accessories, Income, Trips — is saved on your device and works fully offline.

Trips & Mileage
How is overall Mileage calculated?

Total distance driven ÷ total fuel used, across every Fuel log for that vehicle — recalculated fresh each time, never an average of past readings.

What is the Trip tab for?

Logging one named trip's start and stop odometer readings, separate from your regular Fuel logs.

How is a trip's own mileage calculated?

Only when both the start and stop fuel-ups are marked "tank full" — a true brim-to-brim reading. Without both, the trip still shows distance, just no mileage figure, since a partial fill can't be measured accurately.

Can electric vehicles use the Trip tab?

You can still log a trip's distance, but the tank-full toggle doesn't apply to them — tapping it shows a message explaining why, since there's no fuel brim to measure from.

Home Dashboard
What do Total Cost/km and Running Cost/km mean?

Total Cost/km includes everything — Purchase cost, accessories, fuel and maintenance. Running Cost/km covers only fuel and maintenance, so you can see your actual day-to-day running expense on its own.

What is Net Profit?

Income minus Running Cost. It only appears once you've logged at least one Income entry for that vehicle.

Why do my cost figures change when I add an old log entry?

Every figure is calculated fresh from your logs, not stored as a running total — adding or editing any past entry updates every number that depends on it right away.

What does the reminder card on Home show?

Any upcoming maintenance item you've set a due date or due odometer reading for, so you don't need a separate alert for it.

Valuation & Depreciation
How is my vehicle's resale value calculated?

Either automatically, using a yearly depreciation percentage, or by manually entering your own current estimated value whenever you like.

Does the app track when I actually sell a vehicle?

No — resale value is always an estimate for your own reference, with no sold-date or sold-price tracking. Archive or delete the vehicle once it's actually gone.

Why does Valuation need the Manufacture Date?

Depreciation is calculated from the vehicle's age, so a Manufacture Date has to be set first.

Compare
What is Compare?

An opt-in screen where you can see how your vehicle's performance stacks up against other vehicles in your country, and rank against friends you add yourself.

Do I need to sign in to use Compare?

Yes — Compare needs a one-time Google Sign-In, separate from your local PIN. Nothing else in the app needs an account.

What is a Ranked Vehicle?

The one vehicle of yours that friends see when they look at your row. It's picked automatically if you only own one vehicle; otherwise, choose it on the Compare screen.

Why do I need to tap Refresh to see new numbers?

Compare only updates when you tap Refresh — it recalculates your own costs, syncs your percentile and your friends' latest numbers in one go. Nothing updates in the background on its own.

What do the filter chips above the ranking list do?

Tap any of them to cycle through its options: your country or worldwide, Personal/Commercial, fuel type, wheels, and which metric is shown. Your selections are remembered the next time you open Compare.

How do I add friends?

Invite shares your personal code with someone you know — they enter it to send you a request. Match pairs you with someone at random instead.

What does long-pressing a friend's row do?

Opens Challenge, End Challenge, or Unfriend. Challenge resets every month.

What do the badge and stars mean?

Your badge reflects how many friends you have and grows through a set of tiers. The stars on your own vehicle show roughly how you perform against others in your country and vehicle class.

Reports & PDF Export
What's in Reports?

Date-range and comparison views of your costs, plus PDF export of either one.

Do I need to pay to use Reports?

No — watch one short ad when you open Reports, and everything inside (both views, unlimited PDF exports) is free to use until you leave the screen. Pro users skip the ad entirely.

Do I need to watch an ad every time I export a PDF?

No — just once per visit to Reports. Export as many PDFs as you like after that, until you leave the screen.

Backup & Restore
How do I back up my data locally?

Go to Profile → Local Backup → Back Up. Choose where to save the file — your PIN protects it, since it's encrypted.

What is Google Cloud Backup?

An automatic backup to your own Google Drive, free for everyone — no Pro or ads required.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account on the new device and restore from Profile → Google Backup, or restore from a local backup file if you made one.

Does restoring a backup also restore my PIN?

Yes — restoring any backup, local or Google, brings back the PIN that was active when that backup was made, along with your data. You'll be asked to unlock again right after.

Security
How do I change my PIN?

Go to Profile → Change PIN, enter your current PIN, then set and confirm a new one.

What happens if I enter the wrong PIN too many times?

The app locks temporarily with a countdown. Wait it out, then try again.

What if I forget my PIN entirely?

If you've taken a backup before, restoring it brings your old PIN back along with your data. If you haven't, your PIN can't be recovered — you'll need to delete your local data and start fresh.

Why does the app still ask for my PIN sometimes, even with biometrics on?

It's a periodic safety check, so you never fully lose track of your real PIN by relying only on biometrics.

Pro & Membership
What does Pro actually unlock?

One thing: skipping the ad on Compare's Refresh and on opening Reports. Every other feature — unlimited vehicles, Google Cloud Backup, Reports and PDF exports — is free for everyone regardless of Pro.

Is Pro a subscription?

Yes, billed annually.

Do I have to watch ads if I don't buy Pro?

Only for Refresh and Reports, and even then it's just one short ad per use.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Go to Profile → Membership → Restore Purchase. It's verified against the Play Store/App Store account signed into that device.

Account & Data
Is an internet connection required to use the app?

No — Garage, logging, Home, Reports (once unlocked), and local Backup/Restore all work fully offline. Google Backup, Compare, and ads need a connection.

Is my data private?

Your vehicle and expense records stay on your device and are never sent anywhere — except the comparison figures you explicitly opt into sharing on Compare, and even then, never your raw records.

What happens if I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account (PIN-protected). This permanently wipes every local record and resets the app to first launch. It doesn't touch your Google Drive backups, and you choose separately whether to also remove your Compare data.
