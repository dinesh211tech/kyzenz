Privacy Policy

MoneyBoss Drive by Kyzenz — Last updated: October 2026

Overview

All your expenses are stored locally on your device and are never seen, collected, or shared by Kyzenz. No account or sign-up is required to use the app.

A few optional features rely on outside services you choose to turn on: Google Drive Backup, Compare (which requires Google Sign-In to rank against friends and other drivers), non-personalized ads to unlock select features for free, and in-app purchases for MoneyBoss Drive PRO. Each is covered under “Optional Features & Third-Party Services” below, along with exactly what each service can and can’t see.

Permissions We Request

MoneyBoss Drive requests the following device permissions, each limited to a single purpose:

  • Notifications — your daily reminder (to log expenses and refresh your Compare rank) is scheduled locally on your device, and automatically rescheduled if your phone restarts. No notification data is sent to Kyzenz servers, and notifications can be disabled anytime in the app settings.
  • Biometric authentication — if you enable fingerprint or face login, authentication happens entirely on your device’s secure hardware. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to MoneyBoss Drive or Kyzenz.

Data We Collect

Your vehicle and expense records are stored locally on your device in a private database. They are:

  • Never uploaded to Kyzenz servers in any identifiable form
  • Never used for advertising or sold to third parties
  • Only accessible to the MoneyBoss Drive app on your device

The app includes no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting services. The only data that ever leaves your device is described below, and only if you choose to turn on Google Drive Backup or Compare.

Optional Features & Third-Party Services

The following features are entirely optional and require you to opt in. Turning one of them on is the only way any of your data ever leaves your device.

Google Drive Backup

You may choose to back up your data to Google Drive. This is entirely optional and user-initiated, and runs automatically once turned on.

  • Backups are stored in your personal Google Drive app data folder, accessible only to MoneyBoss Drive — not your regular Drive files
  • Kyzenz cannot access your Google Drive or backup files
  • You can delete your backup at any time from Google Drive settings

Compare (requires Google Sign-In)

Compare is an optional screen that lets you see how your vehicle’s running costs and mileage stack up against other drivers, and rank against friends you add yourself. Turning it on requires signing in with a Google account, used only to create your Compare identity — Kyzenz never sees your Google password, and sign-in itself is handled entirely by Google.

Compare shares two different kinds of information, and they work differently:

  • Country/vehicle-class percentile — your vehicle’s cost and mileage figures are compared against other MoneyBoss Drive users in your country with a similar vehicle (same wheel count, and fuel type for the mileage comparison). This is an anonymous aggregate comparison — no other user can see your individual figures through it, only your own relative standing.
  • Friends you add — if someone you invite, or are matched with, accepts your connection, that specific friend can see your chosen display name, your vehicle’s name, your country, your badge, and your actual cost/mileage/profit figures for the one vehicle you’ve set as your Ranked Vehicle. This is not anonymous between friends — that visibility is the entire point of the feature. You choose who you connect with, and can Unfriend anyone at any time to stop sharing with them.

Your raw fuel, maintenance, accessory, income, and trip records themselves are never sent anywhere — only the aggregated cost-per-km, mileage, and profit figures described above.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free users can watch a short rewarded video ad, powered by Google AdMob, to run a Compare Refresh or open Reports. These ads are non-personalized — Google does not use your browsing history, advertising ID, or a behavioural profile to choose which ad you see, and the ads never see your vehicle or expense data.

You can avoid ads entirely by upgrading to PRO. Learn more at Google’s Partner Sites policy.

In-App Purchases

MoneyBoss Drive PRO is an optional annual subscription available through Google Play and the App Store. Its only benefit is skipping the ad on Compare Refresh and Reports — every other feature, including unlimited vehicles and Google Drive Backup, is free for everyone regardless of PRO status. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play, the App Store, and RevenueCat. We receive only confirmation that a valid purchase has been made — no payment details or personal financial information is shared with Kyzenz.

Compliance & Your Rights

If you’re located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, MoneyBoss Drive shows a consent dialog (via Google’s User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ad, letting you choose whether non-personalized ads can be shown. You can review or change this choice anytime from Profile → Privacy Options.

MoneyBoss Drive is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time from Profile → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile and all records from the device.

If you’ve opted into Compare, Delete Account gives you the option to also remove your Compare data — your display name, vehicle figures, and friend connections — from our servers at the same time. If you skip that option, your Compare data stays associated with your Google account until you sign in again and remove it from Profile, or contact us below.

If you’ve enabled Google Drive Backup, your backup file remains in your Google Drive until you delete it separately from there.
