Why MoneyBoss Drive?

Fed up with apps that trade your data for their profits? MoneyBoss Drive is different. No account, no signup needed, and your raw vehicle records are never shared. Install it, log a fuel-up, close it. Just you, your garage, and a clear picture of what every vehicle actually costs you. Free forever, by design — unlimited vehicles, unlimited logging. Pro is a convenience, never a requirement. Every feature is unlockable at no cost.

Free

Your Data, Locked Safe

What you spend on your vehicles is nobody's business. Set a PIN and enable biometric login — fingerprint or face. After repeated wrong attempts the app locks automatically. Your records stay private and secure even if your phone is shared or lost.

Free

Dashboard

Open the app and instantly know where you stand — Total Cost/km, Running Cost/km, and Net Profit/km if you log income. Every figure is calculated fresh from your logs, so editing an old entry updates every number that depends on it right away. Upcoming maintenance due by date or odometer shows right on the card — no separate reminder needed.

Free

Add Expenses & Income

No friction, no faff. Fuel, Maintenance (charges, service, papers), Accessories, and Income each get their own tab, with your recent entries right below. Driving for a living? Log income too and Net Profit shows up automatically. Know if you really profit by driving. Tracking a vehicle has never felt this effortless.

Free

Trips & True Mileage

Overall mileage is total distance ÷ total fuel, recalculated fresh every time. Want a single trip's real figure? Log its start and stop odometer readings in the Trip tab — when both fill-ups are a true brim-to-brim "tank full", you get a genuine trip mileage, not an estimate.

Free

Garage

As many vehicles as you like — free, no limit. Full specifications, purchase details, and a resale valuation that updates automatically from a depreciation rate or your own manual estimate. Swap between vehicles anywhere in the app with one swipe on the vehicle image. Sold a vehicle? Archive it and every record stays safe, or delete it permanently with your PIN.

Pro

Compare

See how your vehicle performs against others of the same vehicle class in your country, and rank against friends you add yourself. Invite someone directly, or get matched at random. Refresh any time — free with a short rewarded ad, or skip the ad entirely with Pro. Nothing leaves your device except the comparison figures you explicitly opt into sharing.

Pro

Reports & PDF Export

Your entire cost history, filterable by date range, or compare two vehicles side by side. Export a detailed PDF whenever you need one. Watch one short ad to unlock the screen, then everything inside — both views, unlimited exports — is free until you leave. Pro skips the ad entirely.

Free

Profile

Forgetting to log a fuel-up? Set a daily reminder. Want faster login? Enable biometrics. Worried about losing your data? Back up locally or enable free automatic Google Backup. App not feeling right? Tweak the language or theme. Need a clean slate? Wipe everything in one tap. Your app, your rules — all in one place.

About

Kyzenz builds simple, private apps for everyday life. We believe your data belongs to you — only you.

MoneyBoss Drive is our second app, built the same way as the first: privacy, security, and simplicity as core principles. No account or signup needed, works fully offline for everyday tracking, and your vehicle records are never tracked, monitored, or shared — the only exception is the comparison figures you explicitly choose to share on Compare, and even then, never your raw records.
