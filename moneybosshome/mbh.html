Why MoneyBoss Home?

Fed up with apps that trade your data for their profits? MoneyBoss Home is different. No account, no ads, no data shared — ever. Install it, track your spending, close it. Just you, your phone, and a clear picture of your spending. Core features always free. Pro features optional.

Free

Your Data, Locked Safe

Your spending is nobody's business. Set a PIN and enable biometric login — fingerprint or face. After repeated wrong attempts the app locks automatically. Your expenses stay private and secure even if your phone is shared or lost.

Free

Dashboard

Open the app and instantly know where you stand — monthly total, budget progress, top spends, and 12 months of history. Set a budget and watch it track in real time. Tap a pie slice to drill into any category. Swipe Top Spends for deeper insights. Your money, finally making sense — all signal, no noise.

Free

Add Expense

No friction, no faff. Pick a category, type the amount, done — in under ten seconds. Built-in calculator, smart item suggestions, account tagging, collapsible categories, favourites and your recent expenses — all in one screen. Tracking money has never felt this effortless.

Free

Search & Filter

Every expense you've logged is one scroll away — your recent list. Search by item, filter by category, account, priority, people or notes — and tap any row to edit instantly. Full control, zero friction.

Pro

Family Sharing

See what the whole family spent — not just your half. Each member generates a QR, you scan it, and in seconds you have a combined family report. No internet. No server. No shared account. No company in the middle. Just a scan and a completely private family picture. Fully offline, by design.

Pro

Custom Categories

Tired of squeezing your spending into categories that don't fit? Build your own — groups, categories, item suggestions, in any order you like. Drag to reorder. Your expenses, organised your way.

Pro

Custom Tags

Cash or card? You or the family? Need or want? Tag every expense your way with custom account and people labels. Filter by any combination — instantly.

Pro

Reports & PDF

Your entire spending history, filterable by anything — date, category, account, people, priority, even notes. Compare any two periods side by side. Switch to the Family tab to see everyone's spending combined. Export a detailed PDF grouped by category. The last expense report tool you'll ever need.

Free

Profile

Forgetting to log expenses? Set a daily reminder. Want faster login? Enable biometrics. Worried about losing your data? Manually backup locally or enable Auto backup to Google. App not feeling right? Tweak the language, currency, or theme. Need a clean slate? Wipe everything in one tap. Your app, your rules — all in one place.
