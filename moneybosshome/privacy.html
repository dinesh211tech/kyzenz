Privacy Policy

MoneyBoss Home by Kyzenz — Last updated: September 2026

Overview

MoneyBoss Home is designed to be private first and offline first. All your expenses are stored locally on your device and are never seen, collected, or shared by Kyzenz. No account or signup is required to use the app.

A few optional features rely on outside services you choose to turn on: Google Drive Backup, non-personalized ads to unlock select features for free, and in-app purchases for MoneyBoss Home PRO. Each is covered under “Optional Features & Third-Party Services” below, along with exactly what each service can and can’t see.

Permissions We Request

MoneyBoss Home requests the following device permissions, each limited to a single purpose:

  • Notifications — daily reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to Kyzenz servers, and notifications can be disabled anytime in the app settings.
  • Biometric authentication — if you enable fingerprint or face login, authentication happens entirely on your device's secure hardware. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to MoneyBoss Home or Kyzenz.
  • Camera — used only to scan QR codes for Family Sharing. No photos or images are stored or transmitted. Denying this permission does not affect any other feature.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. MoneyBoss Home does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to function. The app includes no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting services.

All expense data is stored locally on your device in a private database.

  • Never uploaded to Kyzenz servers
  • Never used for analytics or advertising
  • Only accessible to the MoneyBoss Home app on your device

Optional Features & Third-Party Services

The following features are entirely optional and require you to opt in. Turning one of them on is the only way any of your data ever leaves your device.

Google Drive Backup

You may choose to back up your data to Google Drive. This is entirely optional and user-initiated.

  • Backups are stored in your personal Google Drive app data folder, accessible only to MoneyBoss Home
  • Kyzenz cannot access your Google Drive or backup files
  • You can delete your backup at any time from Google Drive settings

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MoneyBoss Home offers a free way to unlock some features without a paid subscription: watching a short rewarded video ad, powered by Google AdMob. These ads are non-personalized — Google does not use your browsing history, advertising ID, or a behavioral profile to choose which ad you see, and the ads never see your expense data.

Showing an ad does require Google's AdMob SDK to make its own network requests to load and serve that ad. You can avoid ads entirely by upgrading to PRO, or simply not using the ad-unlock option. Learn more at Google's Partner Sites policy.

In-App Purchases

MoneyBoss Home PRO is an optional subscription available through Google Play and the App Store. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play, the App Store, and RevenueCat. We receive only confirmation that a valid subscription is active — no payment details or personal financial information is shared with Kyzenz.

Compliance & Your Rights

If you're located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, MoneyBoss Home shows a consent dialog (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ad, letting you choose whether non-personalized ads can be shown. You can review or change this choice anytime from Profile → Privacy Options.

MoneyBoss Home is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time from Profile → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile and all data from the device.

If you've enabled Google Drive Backup, your backup file remains in your Google Drive until you delete it separately from there.
